# Email Receipt (購買後感謝信)

請將以下英文內容直接複製貼上到 Lemon Squeezy 的「Thank you note」欄位：

```text
Thank you for purchasing the Vajra Claw Hacker Tier!

Your exclusive License Key is included in this email.

📥 Software Download: [Please insert your Google Drive or official download link here]
📖 Official Documentation: https://dr-os.io/#integration

If you have any questions or need technical support, please feel free to reach out to us at:
service@dr-os.io
```

## 結帳後按鈕設定 (Button Settings)

- **Button text**: `Access Documentation`
